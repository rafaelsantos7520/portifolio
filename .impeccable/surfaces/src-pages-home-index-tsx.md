# Home

Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience: recruiter or collaborator on a short hiring visit. Job: decide whether Rafael is worth a conversation. Action: open a live project or a contact channel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one coffee-export sack on a dock table — Rafael's name is the stencil mark, his three apps are lots stamped at unequal scale, and contact is the tag on the twine. It refuses the dark-hero / round-avatar / equal-card-grid template.

OWN-WORLD: Cool oatmeal jute ground, carbon stencil ink, one aniline export-blue accent. Condensed industrial display (Big Shoulders Display) against Atkinson Hyperlegible for prose. Grouping is stamp blocks, dashed stitch rules, and scale — never identical rounded cards. Fiber noise in the ground. Focus is a blue stencil box.

STORY: The visitor reads who this is from the stencil, sees three real lots, opens a live app or talks on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed sack. Top hem is navigation (Início, Projetos, Origem, Contato) plus a blue-stamped Fale comigo. The name RAFAEL GOMES is the display, two lines, filling the width. A short bio sits under the name in a 65–75ch measure, not in a kicker. The featured lot (MotorsShop) already occupies the lower third of the viewport as a large stamp; the other two lots sit smaller on the same band. Portrait is a small rectangular inked stamp, not a circular hero avatar. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a lot strikes it (ink densifies, others ghost); snap, not tween.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 — Brazilian coffee export sack / lot stencil. Seed key 9ee23f84.

Raises kept from the hand:
- Azulejo seam: layout snaps to an 8px module; sections break on full stitch rules.
- Brick inventory: each project shows language as parts, then the assembled repo/live links.
- Cathode strike: all lots present; the active one is struck; snap not tween.
- Darkroom emerge: scroll reveal is one soak of ink from an already-visible default.
- Bollywood billing: MotorsShop outranks by scale.
- Du Bois form-per-question: stack is a composition strip, projects are lots, contact is a tied tag.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
